🔧 How It Works

ipadScreen runs a small app on your jailbroken iPad. This app receives screen data from your Mac and displays it in real-time. The connection can be either wired (USB) for maximum stability or wireless (Wi-Fi) for convenience.

On the Mac side, the software uses modern screen capture technology (ScreenCaptureKit on newer macOS versions) to grab your screen content and stream it to the iPad. The clever part is that it optimizes for older hardware by using compressed JPEG streams, ensuring your iPad 2 can keep up without lag.

🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting

💡 iPad not connecting via USB? Make sure you're using a data cable, not just a charging cable. Also verify that your iPad is properly jailbroken and the app is installed correctly.
💡 Low frame rate on Wi-Fi? For best performance, especially with older iPads, use a USB connection. If you must use Wi-Fi, try moving closer to your router.
💡 App crashes on iOS 6? Make sure you have the latest version. This project is specifically optimized for iOS 6 and tested on iPad 2, so it should work smoothly on similar hardware.

📊 Performance & Optimization

ipadScreen is built to squeeze every bit of performance out of aging hardware. The magic comes from libjpeg-turbo with NEON optimization, which speeds up image compression significantly on ARM processors like those in the iPad 2.

In real-world testing, users report around 35 frames per second on the iPad 2. That's surprisingly smooth for a device from 2011! The app intelligently balances quality and speed, ensuring you get a usable display without excessive lag.
